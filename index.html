Robot-proactivity annotation, video-grounded

A design draft. Real HD-EPIC actions, filtered to the recipe windows, aligned across sessions on recipe-step completion. Video clips are not wired up yet, so each instance shows its narration in place of the clip.

How to use this, in six steps
  1. Pick a recipe. Two are loaded. The numbers above tell you how many actions there are and how many distinct kinds of action.
  2. Glance at the top panel. It shows the kinds of action that happen in more than one session, placed left to right by where they usually fall.
  3. Look at the lanes. One lane per session, every action shown. The vertical rules are recipe steps, and they line up across sessions even though the sessions took different amounts of time. Drag across the top strip to zoom in; double-click to zoom back out.
  4. Click any block. The panel at the bottom shows what the person was doing, what came just before and after, and how long their hands were free afterwards.
  5. Rate it. Passive, confirm, or assist. Press 1 2 3 and it moves to the next action on its own. Arrow keys move without rating. Rated blocks turn green.
  6. Optional: click a box in the top panel to see just that kind of action across all sessions, and check whether your answer holds when it happens at a different moment. Click it again to go back.
